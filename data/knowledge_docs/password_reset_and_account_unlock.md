# Reset a password or resolve an account lockout

Use only the organization's approved password-reset portal or identity support process. Exact reset and unlock rules vary by organization.

## Steps

1. Open the password-reset portal from a trusted company link or bookmark. Do not follow reset links from unexpected email or chat messages.
2. Complete the portal's identity verification using your enrolled multifactor authentication method.
3. Choose a new password that meets the organization's displayed requirements. Do not reuse a password from another service.
4. If the account is locked, follow the portal's unlock option or wait for the stated lockout period before trying again. Repeated guesses can extend a lockout.
5. After a successful reset, sign in to the required work applications and update the password only in approved device prompts.

## Security reminders

IT will not need your password or one-time verification code. Never send either by email or chat. Do not use unofficial reset tools or attempt to bypass identity verification.

## Escalate to IT

Contact the identity support team if the portal cannot verify you, no enrolled MFA method is available, the account remains locked after the portal's instructions, or you suspect someone else changed your password. Share the error and approximate time, but not the password or verification code.
